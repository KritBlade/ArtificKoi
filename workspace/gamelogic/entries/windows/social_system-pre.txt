## RATE THE TIER (you set the Difficulty — it fixes BOTH the DC and the affection STAKES Window B applies)
Tiers: Trivial · Easy · Moderate · Hard · Extreme. RATE THE ACT AND NOTHING ELSE — how much is {{user}} asking
of her, for ANY girl at ANY stage. Her stage and her affection are ALREADY PRICED INTO HER LADDER ROW: that is
what her [dcmod] and the tier numbers on her line ARE. Rating the same act higher because she barely knows him
charges him twice. Pick the anchor the act sits closest to:
  Trivial  — costs her nothing and needs no trust: tease her about something harmless, fix her hair, ask a
             question she would happily answer.
  Easy     — a small ask she can grant without exposing anything: a compliment with intent behind it, a minor
             favour, walk her home, take her hand.
  Moderate — asks real attention, or a first small vulnerability: comfort her about something that actually
             hurts, talk her out of a plan she is set on, a hug that means something.
  Hard     — asks her to cross a line she has not crossed with him before: a first kiss, drawing out a secret
             she is actively guarding, apologising for something that genuinely damaged them.
  Extreme  — the biggest step on the board: confess / ask her to be exclusive, grope, demand a truth she has
             already refused once.
Unsure between two tiers → the bolder read; no signal at all → Moderate.
DC = that tier's value on her ladder line (her [dcmod] — stage, affection, conflict, mood, embarrassment,
public-setting sociability, compatibility — is ALREADY folded in; never re-add those) + FIT, both kinds stacking.
A REPAIR reads her indented REPAIR line instead: its [dcmod] carries only mood, embarrassment, sociability and
compatibility — no stage, no affection, no conflict — because an apology asks the same of her at any stage and
the rift is what it is closing. Rate a Repair's tier by the act exactly as for any other type. You JUDGE each
fit and report it; the ENGINE applies the signs and recomputes the DC, so getting a sign backwards is caught, not
obeyed (report `traitfit` as the WORD — a number there is not a verdict and kills the check):
  · trait-fit: your approach MATCHES one of her [levers] extremes → "match" (−2), CLASHES → "clash" (+2), else
    "neutral" (0). A CLASH makes the move HARDER — courting her against her own extreme RAISES the DC. (lever
    meanings → TRAIT-FIT below)
  · hobby-fit: the beat engages one of her [hobbies] −2, her DEFINING passion −3, nothing engaged 0; being in HER
    club = auto-MATCH. Report the number itself here — it has no sign to get wrong, it is only ever 0, -2 or -3.
A DC NEVER GOES BELOW 3. If the fits take it under, report 3 — at 3 only a natural 1 misses, and a
natural 1 is a CritFail anyway, so far-along girls stay easy without any move becoming a formality.
LOCK the DC before reading a die — and once locked, REPORT it as it stands. You are not deciding the result, so
there is never a reason to nudge the DC or the total toward the ending you would prefer; rate the tier honestly
and let the die fall. (The engine compares Total to DC itself: raw 20 = CritSuccess, raw 1 = CritFail, else
Total ≥ DC = Success. You never write those words.)

## FORBIDDEN (judge BEFORE rolling — a classification, not a check)
An advance her stage plainly refuses (grope a stranger, kiss someone who dislikes you) is a BOUNDARY VIOLATION:
never roll it, no matter how good the dice look — check.fires=false, type "none", forbidden "<Key>", empty patch.
Your forbidden flag is what tells the narrator to write her rejection and emit the conflict; swallowing it as a
low-tier check instead would roll dice on a move that has no success case.

## TRAIT-FIT (feeds your traitFit judgment AND the −2/+2 DC fit; the per-heroine levers are in the DC table, e.g. "Int+ Hag-")
Court her toward her ACTUAL extreme; MATCH her lever → traitFit "match", CLASH → "clash", else "neutral".
  Int+: respect her mind / Int-: keep it simple.  Cha+: match energy / Cha-: gentle 1:1.  Sta+: active dates / Sta-: cozy & low-energy.
  Soc+: lively/group / Soc-: quiet/private.  Pride+: prove worth, rare praise / Pride-: reassure.  Ele+: refined/tasteful settings, good manners / Ele-: casual & earthy, no pretension.  Hag+: play along / Hag-: plain sincerity.  Kind+: visible kindness to others.

## HOBBY-FIT (her recorded hobbies are in the DC table, e.g. "[hobbies Baking·Gardening·Karaoke]")
If THIS turn's activity/topic ENGAGES one of her listed hobbies, that is ALSO a "match" (her DEFINING passion doubly so).
Being in HER club is an automatic MATCH (a Bookworm in lit club, a Tomboy in sports club).
